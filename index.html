    How it is worked out

    • Working hours a month = 720 hours × availability × utilisation.
    • Earning per working hour = output per hour × value of one unit of work, minus running cost per hour (fuel or power, wear parts, operator incentive).
    • Profit per month = working hours × earning per working hour, minus fixed cost (depreciation, crew, interest). Fixed cost is paid even when the machine stands.
    • Spare pays back when working hours × earning per working hour = spare price. Cost per working hour of a spare = (price + earnings lost while fitting) ÷ life in hours.
    • Idle days = delivery date (or today, if not delivered) minus the date the spare was needed, if machines were stopped waiting.
    • Caught up later: every lost-production figure is multiplied by (100% − the share caught up later), set on the Procurement page. Figures as of: the date at the top fixes "today" for all date sums, in your browser only.
    • Domino: a machine down for D days stops the next machine for D minus its buffer days (work in hand). Lost money = idle days × working hours a day × earning per working hour, for every machine stopped.
    • Spares list: reorder level = (used per month ÷ 30) × (lead time + safety days), rounded up. Reorder when stock + on order ≤ reorder level. Suggested order = enough to reach reorder level + 3 months of use. Dead stock = nothing issued for 365 days (or no use). Overstock = above reorder level + 12 months of use; the extra is money locked in the store.
    • ABC analysis: yearly spend = price × used per month × 12. Sort from biggest to smallest. A spare is A if the spares above it make up less than the A% (70% unless changed) of total spend, B if less than the A+B% (90%), otherwise C.
    • Indent warning: hours run now = hours read + days since reading × working hours a day. Wears out in (life − hours run now) ÷ working hours a day. Raise indent by = wear-out date − lead time − safety margin. If it would arrive after wear-out, the idle days are costed with the domino, as for late supplies.
    • Supplier on time % = scored orders delivered by the promised date ÷ scored orders. Penalty (LD) = order value × penalty % × weeks late (part weeks count as full weeks), up to the cap. The 0.5% a week and 10% cap are made-up examples: use your contract terms.
    • "Value of one unit of work" is the part of the mine's earning you credit to that machine. Here it is what the same work would cost if hired out: about Rs 114 per BCM of overburden in total, split as drilling and blasting 15%, loading 35%, hauling 50%.
